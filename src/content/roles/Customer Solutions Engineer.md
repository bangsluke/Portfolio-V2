## Full Role Description

I sit in the Customer Success team at Acre, a fintech building an end-to-end management system for the UK's £1.4 trillion mortgage market.

Owning the [[Acre Software|Acre]] Enterprise branch of the firm, I manage the integrations of the software, working across [[API]], [[Zapier]] and [[Power BI]] to support clients in extending the capabilities of working with our software.

Alongside my day to day responsibilities, I also focus on internal process optimisations and documentation, ensuring Customer Success are well equipped with information and others are efficient in the repetitive tasks to focus on bigger tasks.
